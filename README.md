# Jimlog 🏋️‍♂️🍎

Jimlog is a premium, full-stack fitness and nutrition tracking application designed for individuals who demand precision and scientific accuracy in their health journey. By combining advanced workout analytics with AI-driven nutrition tracking, Jimlog provides a comprehensive view of your physical performance and dietary health.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Intelligent Workout Tracking
- **Granular Logging**: Track every exercise with specific sets, reps, and weight distributions.
- **Progressive Overload Analytics**: Visualize your strength gains over time with high-fidelity charts.
- **Muscle Group Distribution**: Understand your training volume across different muscle groups to ensure a balanced physique.

### 2. AI-Powered Nutrition Intelligence
- **Natural Language Logging**: Tell Jimlog what you ate (e.g., "3 boiled eggs and a slice of whole-wheat toast") and let our AI handle the caloric and macronutrient breakdown. 🤖
- **Accordion Organization**: View your daily intake grouped intuitively by Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks.
- **Today's Progress Snapshot**: Real-time progress bars for Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fats, and Fiber.

### 3. Advanced Health Analytics
- **Dynamic Charting**: Toggle between Workout and Food Analytics to see the full picture of your progress.
- **Trend Visualization**: Analyze weekly and monthly trends in calorie intake vs. performance goals.

## 🎯 Our Focus on Accuracy

Accuracy is at the core of Jimlog's philosophy. We move beyond simple "estimated guesses" to provide data you can trust:

- **Evidence-Based Calculations**: We use the **Mifflin-St Jeor Formula**—the gold standard in clinical nutrition—to calculate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR).
- **Metric Precision**: Every chart and calculation is handled with high floating-point precision, ensuring that "Today's Progress" and historical trends are mathematically sound. No rounded artifacts or hidden gaps.
- **Goal-Specified Targets**: Macronutrient targets (Protein, Carbs, Fats) are dynamically recalculated based on your specific health goal:
  - *Weight Loss* (controlled deficit)
  - *Maintenance* (stability focus)
  - *Muscle Gain* (optimized surplus and protein distribution)
  - *Extreme Weight Loss* (monitored aggressive deficit)
- **Visual Goal Benchmarking**: Every nutrition chart includes a precise, numeric reference line based on your personal profile, so you always know exactly how close you are to your scientific target.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Shadcn UI.
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, MongoDB.
- **Visualization**: Recharts for high-fidelity data rendering.
- **AI Integration**: Natural language processing for nutritional analysis.
